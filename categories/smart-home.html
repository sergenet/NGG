Smart Home

The Best Smart Home Gadgets & Connected Tech

From voice assistants to security cameras — expert-tested reviews to help you build a smarter, safer, more convenient home.

Disclosure: We may earn a commission when you buy through our links, at no extra cost to you. Learn more.

Smart home technology has evolved from a novelty into a genuine quality-of-life upgrade. Today's connected devices can save you time, reduce energy bills, improve home security, and automate the mundane tasks that eat into your day. But with thousands of products from dozens of brands, choosing the right devices — and making sure they work together — can be overwhelming.

That's where we come in. Our smart home coverage focuses on practical, real-world testing. We don't just read spec sheets — we install these devices in our own homes, live with them for weeks, and evaluate how well they perform in everyday scenarios. We test voice control reliability, app responsiveness, integration with other ecosystems, and long-term durability.

We organize our smart home guides into four key areas: smart speakers & displays (the brain of your connected home), security cameras & doorbells (protecting what matters most), smart lighting (mood, automation, and energy savings), and robot vacuums (the ultimate time-saver for busy households).

Whether you're building a smart home from scratch or upgrading a single room, our guides help you avoid the common pitfalls: ecosystem lock-in, subscription traps, and products that look great in ads but frustrate in practice. We always recommend the best product for the job — not the one with the biggest marketing budget.
