# Traffic research: first three topic clusters

Date: 2026-08-23

Status: demand hypotheses to validate with Search Console impressions. No fabricated search-volume figures are attached.

## Selection logic

The first clusters use audiences already represented in the catalog, have clear gift intent, support multiple non-duplicative decisions, and fit Pinterest's visual discovery behavior. Every proposed page must add compatibility, constraints, tradeoffs, or original experience—not merely repeat a product list.

## Validation rules once Search Console is connected

- Promote a query to a dedicated article only when its intent differs materially from the pillar page.
- Improve titles/snippets where impressions accumulate but CTR is weak.
- Expand pages that earn impressions across several related queries instead of creating near-duplicates.
- Do not change dates unless content is substantively updated.
- Record affiliate clicks and ordered items before calling a traffic topic commercially successful.
