## Content Workflow

### Per-Article Process
1. **Research** (2 hrs): Keyword research, competitor analysis, spec collection
2. **Outline** (1 hr): H-tag structure, product selection, CTA placement
3. **Draft** (3–4 hrs): Write full article following template structure
4. **Media** (1 hr): Product images, comparison tables, infographics
5. **SEO Review** (30 min): Meta tags, schema markup, internal links
6. **Publish & Promote** (30 min): Social sharing, email newsletter mention

### Update Schedule
- **Monthly:** Check for price changes and stock availability
- **Quarterly:** Full content review, re-rate products, add new models
- **Annually:** Complete rewrite of foundational roundup posts

## Monetization Strategy

### Affiliate Networks
1. **Amazon Associates** (Primary) — Product links, comparison tables
2. **ClickBank** — Digital productivity courses, kitchen recipe programs
3. **ShareASale/CJ** — Direct brand partnerships (FlexiSpot, Breville, etc.)

### Revenue Points per Article
- **Roundup posts:** 8–12 affiliate links per article (highest conversion)
- **Single reviews:** 3–5 links (high intent, moderate volume)
- **Comparisons:** 4–6 links (high conversion on "vs" queries)
- **Gift guides:** 10–20 links (seasonal traffic spikes)

### Conversion Optimization
- Place primary CTA above the fold
- Use "Check Price on Amazon →" as default CTA text
- Add urgency where appropriate ("Limited time deal")
- Use comparison tables (highest CTR format for affiliate content)
- Implement "Our #1 Pick" badges to guide decision-making
