# Stage 1 public claim audit

Audit date: 2026-08-25

Scope: public Next.js pages, all 20 generated guide records, all 75 catalog records, the downloadable checklist, and the generated static export. “Evidence” below means the basis available in this repository; it does not imply independent laboratory or hands-on testing.

## Partnership page

| Previous claim | Disposition | Evidence or replacement boundary |
|---|---|---|
| “Independent Reviews. Engaged Audience. Real Results.” | Removed | No current evidence report supported engagement or results. Replaced with a factual statement that relationships are being rebuilt. |
| Sponsored, honest, hands-on reviews | Removed as a current service promise | No documented hands-on program or completed tests. Page now says testing may be claimed only after a documented test. |
| Newsletter sponsorship with “40%+ open rates” | Removed | No current matching-window email report supports the rate. Newsletter sponsorship is inquiry-only and conditional on verified eligibility and performance data. |
| YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels | Removed | No maintained delivery workflow or current service evidence. Page explicitly says social-production packages are not offered. |
| Ages 25-45; 60% male / 40% female; $60K-$150K income | Removed | No current first-party demographic evidence. Page explicitly declines to publish demographics without a current report. |
| Organic 40%, Pinterest 25%, YouTube 20%, Direct 15% | Removed | No matching-window analytics evidence. Page explicitly declines to publish traffic mix without a current report. |
| Minimum two-week testing | Removed | No documented testing operation supports a universal minimum. |
| “Highly-qualified buyers” and current rate card CTA | Removed | No conversion/audience evidence or approved rate card. Replaced with a request for a specific proposal and written confirmation of availability/pricing. |

## Guide audit

Every generated guide displays “Research-based guide” and an adjacent statement that the shortlist uses published product information and catalog data, not a hands-on test. The following high-risk introductions were changed:

| Guide | High-risk prior language | Disposition and basis |
|---|---|---|
| Best Standing Desks of 2026 | Implied a definitive best list | Reframed as comparison of published specifications, adjustment ranges, fit questions, and ownership considerations. |
| Best Ergonomic Chairs | Broad premium-comfort/value conclusion | Reframed around personal fit and published adjustments; added medical/in-person fit boundary. |
| Best Smart Speakers | “command center,” “worth your money” | Replaced with compatibility, features, privacy, and ecosystem decision criteria. |
| Best Air Fryers of 2026 | “revolutionized home cooking” | Replaced with published capacity, controls, cleaning, space, and safety checks. |
| Best Kitchen Gadgets 2026 UPDATED | “viral” and “saving us time” | Removed popularity and first-person performance implications; retained distinct-use comparison. |
| Top Smart Home Tech 2026 | Devices that “know” the user | Replaced with published features, compatibility, privacy, and setup effort. |
| Best Smart Home Upgrades 2026 | Stair-climbing and exact-presence promotional claims | Replaced with category-neutral compatibility, privacy, subscription, and installation checks. |
| Best Desk Lamps for Home Office 2026 | Prevents eye strain and boosts focus | Removed health/performance outcomes; added room/user variability and no medical/hands-on assessment. |
| Roomba vs Roborock | Claimed a head-to-head test and spotless floors | Replaced with research-based comparison and household-dependent fit factors. |
| Best Smart Light Bulbs | “best ... for every budget” narrative | Replaced with compatibility, brightness, control, hub, privacy, and fixture checks. |
| Ninja vs Vitamix | Lifetime/value verdict and claimed head-to-head test | Replaced with published controls, size, specifications, warranty, and ownership trade-offs. |
| Top 5 Smart Coffee Makers | “top-rated” and flavor conclusion | Replaced with published functions plus subjective-flavor and connected-service caveats. |
| MX Master 3S Review | $99/current-price claim, “resounding yes,” and “100+ hours” | Removed. Explicitly labeled research-based, not hands-on; reframed around fit and compatibility. |
| Smart Home Under $200 | Guaranteed whole-home budget, reliability, future-proofing, and unsafe appliance examples | Reframed $200 as a non-guaranteed planning target; added cost, compatibility, privacy, and electrical-safety conditions. |
| Digital Nomad Gadgets | Guaranteed laptop runtime, essentiality, durability, and noise-control language | Replaced with battery, airline, backup, durability-limit, comfort, and variability checks. |
| ShadowAuto vs ShadowTrack | Theft-prevention, “critical,” user-friendly, real-time/instant, feature and battery conclusions | Replaced with published-feature framing, no prevention/recovery guarantee, law/consent, coverage, subscription, battery, and compatibility checks. |

Retained titles containing “Best,” “Top,” “Review,” or “Professional” are legacy route/editorial labels or official product-name terms, not proof of an objective winner. Their pages immediately identify the work as research-based, present trade-offs, and instruct readers to verify current details. Changing those titles would also change established static routes and canonical URLs; Stage 1 therefore corrected the substantive claims without creating unnecessary URL churn.

## Catalog audit

All 75 catalog records were checked by the automated validator. Most records already use catalog-level fit, compatibility, pros/cons, and “last updated” framing. The following descriptions required explicit correction:

- `ergear-single-monitor-arm`: removed “top-rated” and effortless-performance wording; retained only mechanism and published limit checks.
- `irobot-roomba-j9-series`: removed an outcome guarantee; added floor-plan, surface, obstacle, maintenance, and model dependency.
- `vivo-dual-monitor-desk-mount`: removed “perfect” and unsupported audience/value language; retained published mounting requirements.
- `samsung-t7-shield-1tb`: removed unbounded speed/ruggedness language; tied resistance ratings to manufacturer test conditions.
- `shadowauto-obd-ii-gps-car-tracker`: removed promotional immediacy and zero-effort language; added compatibility, coverage, subscription, consent, and legal checks.
- `fullstar-vegetable-chopper-mandoline-slicer-9-in-1`: removed “viral” and speed outcome; added blade safety, cleaning, and storage.
- `furbo-360-dog-camera`: removed pet safety/happiness outcome; retained published functions with subscription/privacy/suitability checks.
- `neakasa-p1-pro-pet-grooming-vacuum`: removed mess and 99% capture outcomes; added pet-specific safety and maintenance checks.
- `ninja-professional-blender-bl610`: retained “Professional” only as part of the official product name; changed the description to a published motor rating and supported tasks.
- `thermopro-twin-tempspike-plus`: retained “booster” only as the manufacturer’s hardware-component label and explicitly qualifies range as published and condition-dependent.

The product page’s generic fallback pros/cons were also replaced. It can no longer invent “exceptional build quality,” “seamless integration,” or “long-term durability” when source data is missing.

## Checklist audit

The previous checklist was fully replaced because the unsupported claims and rigid rules were structural rather than isolated:

- removed “hundreds of hours testing gadgets,” “honest, hands-on reviews,” and “expertly curated” claims;
- removed Amazon-specific return-window advice;
- removed fixed 8-of-10, 3-times-per-week, 10-review, 90%-of-value, 12-month obsolescence, rumor-site, and 48-hour rules;
- removed statements treating low-star reviews as proof of a design flaw;
- added retailer-neutral checks for compatibility, total ownership cost, seller/return/warranty terms, evidence quality, privacy, safety, accessibility, support, repair, and end of life;
- added an explicit research-based/not-hands-on boundary and a reusable decision record.

Source: `2026 GADGET BUYER.docx`. Public download: `public/downloads/2026-gadget-buyers-checklist.pdf`.

## Trust and policy pages

- Affiliate disclosure retains the factual Amazon closure statement and makes clear that retailer-controlled terms change.
- Editorial policy now defines research inputs, sponsored-content/sample disclosures, sponsor boundaries, current-price limitations, and correction handling.
- Privacy policy now avoids hard-coding a newsletter vendor, explains list use, adds request verification, retention, and service-provider boundaries, and does not overstate data-sale practices.
- Terms and contact pages retain general informational, changing-product, correction-source, partnership-arrangement, and disclosure boundaries.

## Automated enforcement

`scripts/validate-truthmode.mjs` checks all 20 guide records, all 75 products, the partnership source, the closed Store ID boundary, undocumented testing phrases, health outcomes, guaranteed outcomes, unsupported rankings/verdicts, and reliability/durability claims. It runs inside `npm run validate:data`, and therefore inside both `npm run check` and `npm run build`.
