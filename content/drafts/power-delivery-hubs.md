Modern laptops like the MacBook Pro have incredible performance but often lack the necessary ports for a productive desktop setup. A high-quality USB-C or Thunderbolt 4 hub is the centerpiece of any professional home office.

We tested the leading docks on the market for transfer speed, thermal performance, and reliability when driving dual 4K monitors.

## Thunderbolt 4 vs USB-C
If you are running multiple high-resolution displays or transferring massive video files, Thunderbolt 4 is non-negotiable. It provides 40Gbps bandwidth compared to standard USB-C's 10Gbps. However, if you just need a few extra USB-A ports and an HDMI out for travel, a standard USB-C hub will save you hundreds of dollars.

## Power Delivery (PD)
A good hub doesn't just connect peripherals—it powers your laptop. Look for hubs that offer at least 85W of pass-through power delivery. This allows you to use a single cable to connect your laptop to your monitor, keyboard, mouse, and power grid.
