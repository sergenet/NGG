Whether you want to protect your personal vehicle from theft, keep an eye on a teen driver, or secure expensive tools on a job site, a reliable GPS tracker is a critical security upgrade. Today's smart trackers combine real-time GPS coordinates with instant mobile alerts and geofencing to give you complete peace of mind.

In this guide, we break down the two flagship devices from ShadowGPS: the **ShadowAuto OBD-II Car Tracker** and the **ShadowTrack Portable GPS Asset Tracker**. Both integrate with the same user-friendly ShadowGPS app, but they serve different security needs.

## ShadowAuto vs. ShadowTrack: Quick Comparison

Here is how the two main options compare at a glance:

- **Power Source**: ShadowAuto runs on vehicle power via the OBD-II port. ShadowTrack is wireless with a 15-day rechargeable battery.
- **Installation**: ShadowAuto plugs into standard OBD-II ports (under the dashboard). ShadowTrack mounts magnetically to any flat steel surface.
- **Diagnostics**: ShadowAuto reads vehicle health codes and fuel/battery levels. ShadowTrack is location-only.
- **Best For**: ShadowAuto is best for cars, trucks, and fleets. ShadowTrack is best for trailers, gear boxes, boats, and covert placement.

## How to Choose the Right GPS Tracker for Your Setup

### Choose the ShadowAuto if:
You need a permanent tracking solution for a standard passenger car or fleet vehicle. Because it plugs directly into the OBD-II port, you never have to worry about charging batteries, and it can read direct dashboard diagnostic data to warn you about engine trouble before a breakdown.

### Choose the ShadowTrack if:
You need to track assets without standard OBD-II ports, like landscaping trailers, boats, outdoor gear, or construction equipment. Its strong magnetic mount and long-lasting battery make it easy to conceal in seconds.
